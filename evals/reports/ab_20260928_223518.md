## Fusion mechanism (gold rank: vector vs lexical vs fused)

- queries with gold in BOTH channels: 59
- lexical rank is WORSE than vector rank on: **15** / 59 (25%)
- fusion DRAGGED the gold below its vector rank: **11**
- fusion LIFTED the gold above its vector rank: 9
- fusion left it flat: 39
- mean rank of gold: vector **1.53**, lexical **2.63**, fused **1.69**
- **mechanism**: the lexical opinion is weaker on average (mean rank 2.63 vs 1.53), and RRF averages ranks - so the fused rank (1.69) lands between them, i.e. BELOW where vectors alone had the gold. 11 demotions vs 9 promotions. This is why RRF k and BM25 score gates cannot help: they do not change either channel's rank ORDER, and order is all RRF reads. The only fusion-side lever is the lexical WEIGHT (or a cross-encoder that re-scores from scratch).

## Verdict

- shipped hybrid vs vector_only: recall@5 0.983 -> 0.950 (**-0.033**), recall@1 0.742 -> 0.708 (**-0.033**)
- best NON-ablation config: **`hybrid_rrf60+rerank`** recall@5=0.975 mrr=0.942 recall@1=0.908 (vs vector_only 0.983/0.853/0.742)
- does any fusion beat pure vector on recall@5? **NO**
- ...but it wins on MRR (0.942 vs 0.853) and recall@1 (0.908 vs 0.742): it finds the gold slightly less often inside top-5, but ranks it clearly better when it does. For a k=5 reader that is a real win.
- BM25 unique coverage (queries only the lexical channel finds): **0** / 60
- mechanism: with zero unique lexical coverage, RRF can only reorder the vector channel's own candidates using a second opinion that never adds a correct one. Hybrid losing to vector_only is then EXPECTED, not a bug. Ranked by what the sweep measured: a cross-encoder FIXES it (it re-scores from scratch), a lower lexical weight MITIGATES it, and RRF k / a BM25 score floor do NOTHING (both leave rank order intact, and rank order is all RRF reads).

- action: `hybrid_rrf60+rerank` is the candidate to promote. Before it replaces the shipped default it must (a) win the per-case diff, not just the mean - a mean can hide 8 wins / 8 losses, and (b) be re-measured on a SECOND corpus, because this one is 90% homogeneous CSV rows.
- evidence in one line: gold's mean rank is 1.53 in the vector channel and 2.63 in BM25, with 0 unique lexical finds over 60 queries.
- scope warning: this is ONE corpus dominated by 351 homogeneous CSV rows. A conclusion of 'BM25 is useless' would be over-fitting; the defensible conclusion is about the FUSION RULE (unweighted RRF treats a weaker channel as an equal opinion), which is corpus-independent.
