- queries with gold in BOTH channels: 59

- mean rank of gold: vector **1.53**, lexical **2.63**, fused **1.68**
- **mechanism**: the lexical opinion is weaker on average (mean rank 2.63 vs 1.53), and RRF averages ranks - so the fused rank (1.68) lands between them, i.e. BELOW where vectors alone had the gold. 11 demotions vs 9 promotions. This is why RRF k and BM25 score gates cannot help: they do not change either channel's rank ORDER, and order is all RRF reads. The only fusion-side lever is the lexical WEIGHT (or a cross-encoder that re-scores from scratch).
